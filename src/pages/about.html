The beginning

Where it all began

Kirirom, Cambodia · class of 2019

My story with technology starts at Kirirom Institute of Technology, a campus in the pine forests of Kirirom, where I studied Software Engineering.

It's also where Japan came into my life: an exchange program in Kyoto that was my very first flight abroad, and a three-month internship in Tokyo in my third year.

I graduated in 2019, spent four years as a project manager, and in 2024 found my way into a role between IT and media. It suits me: part of the day solving problems, part of the day making things read and look right.

The turning point

“The moment I realised the details nobody notices are what make something feel right.”

Editing our product catalogue was a quiet job: checking every name, price and photo, page after page. When it earned me a Good Job Award from my company, I saw that the careful, invisible work counts too. Now I don't just fix things. I try to make them clear for the people who use them.
